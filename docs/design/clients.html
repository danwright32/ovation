Ovation, round 3: what a count does when it is nothing

Both, and the sidebar line only when it has something to say.

Round 2 settled on R2 and R3 together: the amount rides beside the client in the list, so you can see which clients without clicking any of them, and a line in the sidebar card carries it to every screen. Both are now in every rendering below and neither is a variable any more.

Dan added a rule with it: it should not say Money held 0. That is a rule rather than a case, and nothing in the PRD or the design record has ever decided what a count does when it is nothing, so it lands on a card that already has four other lines. This round moves one variable: which lines that rule applies to, and what is left when it takes them all.

Use the day switch. The three positions are identical on a busy day and differ only on a quiet one, which is the day the rule is about. A quiet day is not rare: it is every day the work is done, which is the state the product should be nicest in.

The card line carries the AMOUNT, not a count of clients, and that is a judgement rather than a settled decision. Three clients holding $87.50 between them is not worth a line; one client holding $5,000.00 is, and a count of 3 cannot tell those apart. It costs consistency: the four lines beside it are counts of things, and this one is money. Say if you want the count instead.

Settled and no longer moving

The two pane shape. The amount beside the client in the list. A money held line in the sidebar card. All three are in every rendering here.

What an empty card would mean

A screen that draws nothing on the healthy day is indistinguishable from one that failed to draw, and that is the whole argument against Z2. It is also the argument for it: nothing on the screen is the truest possible statement that there is nothing to do.

The cost nobody mentions

Lines that come and go make the sidebar change height as you work, so Invoices, Expenses and Clients sit somewhere different depending on how much is waiting. Measured between a busy day and a quiet one: 26px, 180px and 106px. None of the three holds perfectly still, because the money line comes and goes in all of them.

Not in this round

Whether a deposit is distinguishable from an overpayment (ovation#96), and how held money is kept from reading as one balance with referral credit (PRD 5.14c). Both are next.

What to look for

  1. Switch to a quiet day and look at the sidebar. That is the only place these three differ.
  2. Does an empty card read as calm, or as broken? That is the question.
  3. How much movement is acceptable? None of the three holds perfectly still: the navigation shifts 26px, 180px and 106px respectively between a busy day and a quiet one.
  4. Is Money held $1,837.50 right in a column of counts, or should it be 3?
