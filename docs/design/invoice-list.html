Ovation, the rail

C1 and C2 together

The colour lives permanently in the sidebar, and the row you are on picks it up from there. The list itself stays bare until you touch it, so nothing competes with a record and the colour is still present the moment you open the app.

A rail is roughly twenty times the area of the five thin bands, so the colour is worth reopening rather than assuming espresso survives the change of dose. Six rails, generated with every interior token computed from the rail colour. 72 contrast checks, all clearing the floor, worst 4.83.

The shell

One window, three places: Invoices, Expenses, Clients. Export and Import are File menu commands because they are actions rather than rooms, and Settings is in the app menu where macOS puts it. Requirement 41b makes single window a hard rule, asserted by a test.

No instruction headings

Your call, and the strongest cut in the whole session. The chip said SEND on the left while the action said Send on the right, and the heading above said it a third time. The row already carries draft or number, the shoot date, the days late and the exact action, which covers every case the headings were spelling out.

The row

One line, client and shoot name together, then date, invoice number, amount, action. About a third shorter than the two line version. Venue and shoot times are deliberately absent and need a home elsewhere, filed as ovation#95.

The actions

Words, not buttons. Eight filled buttons beside five filled bands made structure and action look identical. A button also says what it does: Mark cleared, Mark sent.

Downbeat already ships a three tier button system whose plain tier is a word with padding and no border, so this builds natively with nothing invented.

The counts

Four, in the sidebar card, each shown once. Down from six with one appearing twice. Inventory counts are gone: a number in the chrome only ever means this many things need you.

The card and any group total must be computed once and read twice, never derived separately. Recorded on ovation#49.

The idle half

Disclosures, with Sent awaiting payment open. The group you have money riding on stays visible. No "Nothing to do" label above them, because the group names already say it.

Every state stays on this one screen and reachable, which requirement 22b insists on: a state matching no view is invisible rather than merely awkward.

Espresso, and no red

An overdue invoice is not an error. Nothing has failed, the money has not arrived. Lateness is an age in mono, a duration rather than an alarm. Red belongs to the Problems store alone, whose kinds are all faults in the app itself.

Espresso #3B2B21 on the group bands, chosen against ten alternatives and then against a warm to cool ramp.

The foot strip

Names the fault rather than counting it. "Backup failed 3 days ago" tells you what to do; "2 problems" does not.

It also says what looking means: Ovation checks the booking folder and the Gmail label only while it is open, so everything time based is as old as the last launch.

What is left

  1. Where the venue and the times live, ovation#95. The row has no room and the handoff sends both on every booking.
  2. One invoice per shoot or one per run, ovation#97, and blocking. Downbeat sends three bookings for a three night run and nothing says whether that is three invoices.
  3. Two screens. The receipts queue, and review and send, which is the one where a mistake reaches a client.
  4. The icon, ovation#18, which was waiting on exactly this: the palette is now settled, so it can be drawn to sit inside espresso.
